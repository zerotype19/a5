# Draft go-to-market pilot — not activated

Launch only after READINESS.md gates are satisfied. No account, campaign, spend or outreach was changed by this task.

1. Select two or three services with an active accepting provider, backup plan, attractive A5 economics and evidence. Masonry, handyman and painting are draft examples, not a determination of supply or search demand.
2. Limit location targeting to confirmed availability within Florham Park, Madison, Chatham, Morris Township, Morristown and East Hanover. Select people present in the target area where platform settings allow it. Exclude unsupported geography and services. Do not imply an office in every town.
3. Start with high-intent exact/phrase service queries. Separate ad groups by service. Use matching landing pages with campaign tags. The CSV contains copy/keyword drafts, not a platform import file or launch authorization.
4. Set an owner-approved account/campaign cap and staffed-hour schedule. Keep emergency/24-hour keywords out unless operations are changed and approved. Review search terms and lead quality closely during the first week.
5. Use contextual request links and a visible phone alternative. Do not advertise “licensed,” “insured,” “same day,” “free quote,” “guaranteed,” or “top rated” without evidence supporting the specific claim and business role.
6. Record the complete lead-to-job chain and reconcile actual spend with qualified leads and A5 contribution. Apply the pre-agreed loss ceiling before scaling. Expand service/town coverage only when provider capacity and economics allow it.

Shared negative-query candidates for review: jobs, careers, salary, apprenticeship, training, course, DIY, tutorial, wholesale, supplies, emergency, 24 hour. Review exclusions carefully; never blindly exclude a useful query based on a single word.

Partner channel: real estate agents, property managers and complementary providers can be potential sources. First define an appropriate relationship and disclosure with the owner. Use tracked referral links such as ?utm_source=partner_slug&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=local_pilot (no names/emails in tags). Prepare outreach only to identified appropriate recipients and send only when specifically authorized. Do not scrape or activate providers.

Google Business Profile is conditional: Google's eligibility policy excludes lead-generation businesses. Confirm A5's actual role and in-person operating model before creating or changing a profile. Do not invent storefronts or use virtual offices to simulate local presence. https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036

For AI/search discovery, prioritize crawlable accurate pages, original evidence, reviewed explanations and authentic local mentions. No special AI text file, fake citations or ranking guarantee is part of this package. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features

Weekly decision: keep / improve / pause each service campaign based on lead fit, contact timeliness, provider acceptance, estimate progression, mature wins and acquisition cost. Broad social ads and retargeting are later experiments, after the offer and consent/data practices are verified.
