# Evidence and content production brief

The nine ImageGen assets are illustrative. Do not convert them into project stories, claim the depicted home is in a specific town, or invent before/after results. Asset origins and prompts are recorded in image-sources.json. Current files are optimized local WebP variants in public/images.

## Request from owner/providers

For each approved project: written homeowner/publication permission; provider permission for supplied photography; actual service and town (no street address by default); work date; initial problem; assessed cause; agreed scope; what was performed; limitations; completion outcome; attributable provider; project photos with photographer/rights and consent references. Keep homeowner identifying details private unless separately authorized. Distinguish provider portfolio work from A5-coordinated work.

Photograph the whole area and close-up details, ideally before/after from the same angle with similar light. Avoid license plates, house numbers, faces and private belongings. Supply originals. Do not exaggerate scope or edit images to suggest work not performed.

For reviews: verbatim authorized wording, reviewer attribution preference, source URL/date, what interaction the review actually describes and permission for use. Never import another business's aggregate rating as A5's rating. Request feedback consistently, not only from satisfied customers.

For the owner/team: preferred public name/title, real photo, experience that can be substantiated, actual local connection, contact role and responsibility. For providers: verified registrations/licenses/insurance where applicable, trade/service scope, verification date and who checked them. No blanket “fully vetted” claim without a documented process.

## Reusable project page brief

Title: [Actual service/project] in [approved town].
Opening: what the homeowner needed and who performed the work.
Images: permissioned before/after with factual captions and alt text.
Scope/outcome: concise, specific and reviewed by the responsible provider.
Cost/timing: actual documented figures only with permission and caveats; otherwise explain factors without invented ranges.
Links: relevant published service, problem and town pages.
CTA: contextual /request-service link.
Publishing: follow existing content registry review, explicit public_project_approved and owner publication gates. Operational photos are never automatically public.

## Content queue

First improve the eight existing service pages with genuine evidence. Then review actual lead questions and Search Console demand to prioritize:

- Masonry: repair versus rebuild, repointing scope, what makes an estimate change.
- Handyman: preparing a multi-item list, work requiring a separate trade.
- Painting/drywall: correcting moisture causes before cosmetic work, scope and finish expectations.
- Landscaping: cleanup versus grading requests and what a site visit must establish.
- Tile: availability of matching material, substrate/moisture considerations.
- Plumbing/electrical: safe observations, urgency boundaries and professional assessment.

Use named expert review for substantive safety guidance. Source regulatory statements from official current sources. Do not generate all eight services × six towns by default. A new page needs distinct purpose, real demand and evidence. Cost guides need geography, methodology, source dates and owner review under the existing authority workflow.
