# Search evidence and editorial decisions

Observed November 6 in signed-in Search Console, Web report, three-month selection with actual chart observations September 29–November 3: 1,427 impressions, 2 clicks, 0.1% rounded CTR, average position 57.9. Page rows: running toilet 249 impressions; gutters-cost 141; plumbing-cost 119; brick-step-repair 99; handyman-cost 94; appliance-repair-cost 71; hvac-cost 68. Each of these rows has zero clicks. This is early impression traction, not proof of customer demand, lead volume or an effect from the November 2 changes. Do not compare this window directly with the prior 28-day report as a growth rate.

Prioritize project decisions around the five visible cost categories, then extend the same useful structure to electrical, painting, drywall, cleaning and junk removal. All 16 categories already have cost hubs; adding duplicate category hubs would be counterproductive. New pages answer a narrower scope/pricing decision; existing symptom and preparation articles retain their roles. No town-price permutations.

Sources checked November 6:
- ENERGY STAR maintenance checklist: https://www.energystar.gov/saveathome/heating-cooling/maintenance-checklist — annual pre-season checkups and equipment-specific tasks; paraphrased narrowly.
- EPA WaterSense: https://www.epa.gov/watersense/home-maintenance — leak attention and running-toilet follow-up, not a diagnosis of a particular home.
- EPA lead-safe consumer guidance: https://www.epa.gov/lead/renovation-repair-and-painting-rrp-program-consumers — brief pre-1978 painting caveat.
- EPA moisture guidance: https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home — address moisture before cosmetic repair; no remediation procedure.
- Whirlpool warranty information: https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/kitchen/warranties.html — model-specific terms and checking coverage, not a universal warranty promise.
- FTC contractor guidance: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-home-improvement-scam — search-index excerpt verified the recommendation for written work/material/date/price estimates; full-page fetch timed out. No detailed legal or cancellation claims taken from this source.

The new project pages are original editorial checklists, not summaries of a third-party article. They do not introduce numerical ranges. They link to existing category price references, whose dates and provenance are preserved. HomeGuide gutter/electrical/cleaning/junk and Angi handyman pages were also opened for context; no new price observations are published. Source dates represent access/review, not inferred publication dates.

Link strategy: service and town pages → matching category in cost library → category price reference and focused project guide → relevant service-prefilled intake. Each new project guide has incoming explicit relationships from its service and price hub and links back to both. All category hubs link to the comparison and maintenance guides. Published-only discovery and sitemap rules remain unchanged. New pages should supplement, not replace, the pages already earning impressions.

Measurement: preserve the November 2 cohort baseline. Report this November 6 expansion separately, allowing November 7–December 4 as its first full 28-day window and Search Console reporting lag afterward. Watch URL/query impressions and clicks, intent overlap, and reviewed requests. Consolidate only if later URL-level evidence shows redundant intent. No ranking or lead guarantee.
