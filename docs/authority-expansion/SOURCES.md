# Source ledger

Retrieved October 7, 2026. These sources support the narrow statements below, not vendor availability, A5 project history or blanket permit determinations. General planning recommendations are editorial guidance.

- [ESFI Electrical Safety Dos](https://www.esfi.org/electrical-safety-dos/): Electrical warning signs warrant inspection by a qualified electrician.
- [EPA mold cleanup guidance](https://www.epa.gov/mold/what-are-basic-mold-cleanup-steps): Address water problems rather than covering affected materials with a finish.
- [Madison Building & Construction Department](https://www.rosenet.org/153/Building-Construction-Department): Madison's Building Department covers Madison and Chatham Borough. Use the department for project-specific construction questions.
- [Florham Park permitting](https://www.florhamparknj.gov/Permitting): Florham Park provides permit submission information and inspection request instructions; inspection requests identify the permit, address, inspection type and contact.
- [Chatham Borough planning, zoning and construction](https://www.chathamborough.org/departments/construction): Chatham Borough has its own planning/zoning information and links to the Madison Construction Department and its permit portal.
- [Chatham Township construction](https://chathamtownship.org/directory/construction/): Chatham Township lists a separate construction contact for permits and inspections.
- [Morris Township building and construction](https://www.morristwp.com/121/Building): Morris Township's Building Department handles construction permits and inspections. Its page links to its SDL portal and permit guidance.
- [Morristown building and construction](https://www.townofmorristown.org/building): Morristown's construction office can help determine permit requirements; its building page describes permits, inspections and prior approvals.
- [East Hanover Construction Department](https://www.easthanovertownship.com/Departments/construction-department): East Hanover's construction page states grading approval precedes a building permit when earthwork is involved. It publishes zoning information and separate inspection schedules.
- [EPA Lead Renovation, Repair and Painting Program](https://www.epa.gov/lead/lead-renovation-repair-and-painting-program): Disturbing lead-based paint can create hazardous dust. EPA recommends lead-safe certified contractors for renovation, repair or painting in pre-1978 homes.
