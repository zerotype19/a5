The Essex source is a dated county planning document listing the 18 consortium municipalities and four separate entitlement municipalities. It supports municipal identity only; no current funding rules, demographic statistics or service claims are carried over. Passaic's county business directory similarly supports the municipal inventory, not current business or provider availability. NJ's older municipality-code PDF was not used as the sole inventory because it contains historical municipal entries elsewhere in the state.

Names retain useful borough/township distinctions where names would otherwise collide. Common short municipal names are used where unambiguous within this scope. Washington Township is represented separately for Bergen, Morris and Warren. North Bergen and Union City belong to Hudson; Union Township belongs to Union. Chatham Borough and Chatham Township have distinct new operational IDs; the historical combined `chatham` ID is retained for existing data and its shared authored guide. No historical leads or vendor coverage are remapped.

## Content review

Eight individually authored county pages contain direct answers, practical project-planning guidance, location-disambiguation notes, contextual request links and official references. They do not create town/service/problem permutations, invent office addresses, testimonials, ratings, costs, local housing statistics, permit rules or confirmed vendor coverage.

Editorial themes: Bergen room refresh/punch lists; Essex bathroom and finish repair sequence; Hudson managed-property access; Morris multi-project lists and municipality distinctions; Passaic indoor/outdoor project access; Sussex outdoor assessment preparation; Union occupied-room project staging; Warren repair scope and exterior project coordination. These are suggested planning approaches, not claims about the prevalence of a project or property type in a county.

Existing service pages provide deeper repair/use-case guidance. Existing town hubs remain published and discoverable. All new request areas require individual provider availability review. The original eight service categories are unchanged.
